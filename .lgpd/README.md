**Rules**
- **No personal data in this folder.** It describes data; it never holds any.
- **Not legal advice.** The skills say so and so do we. The privacy policy and the legal bases need a **human check, ideally a lawyer's**, before they are published or relied on.
- Norm texts for citations: [`ctresb/leis`](https://github.com/ctresb/leis); the MDS page on LGPD. Verify an article against the official text before quoting it.
